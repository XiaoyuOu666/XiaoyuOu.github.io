My research connects finite-blocklength communication theory with practical multiuser wireless systems. I study reliability–latency–throughput trade-offs under short-packet constraints and design multiuser MIMO systems for RSMA and NOMA. Across these directions, I use convex optimization techniques to develop low-complexity algorithms for precoding, receive combining, power control, and resource allocation. I implement these algorithms on software-defined radio testbeds to connect theoretical design with over-the-air validation.
