I am a PhD candidate in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at the <a class="academic-link" href="https://www.bristol.ac.uk/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">University of Bristol</a>, United Kingdom, and a member of the <a class="academic-link" href="https://www.bristol.ac.uk/research/groups/csn/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Communication Systems and Networks (CSN) Group</a>. I am supervised by <a class="academic-link" href="https://orcid.org/0000-0002-0018-815X" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Dr. Shuping Dang</a> and <a class="academic-link" href="https://www.bristol.ac.uk/people/person/Angela-Doufexi-97df6244-1c42-44cd-9b5e-79feb29212fe/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Prof. Angela Doufexi</a>.

My research focuses on resource allocation and transceiver design for multiuser MIMO systems in the finite-blocklength regime, with an emphasis on ultra-reliable low-latency communications (URLLC). I use convex optimization techniques to develop low-complexity resource allocation algorithms for multiple access schemes, including rate-splitting multiple access (RSMA) and non-orthogonal multiple access (NOMA). I also work on implementing these algorithms on software-defined radio (SDR) testbeds, connecting theoretical development with over-the-air experimentation.

Before joining Bristol, I received my MSc in Information and Communication Engineering in 2023 and my BEng in Electronic Science and Technology in 2020, both from <a class="academic-link" href="https://english.cqupt.edu.cn/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Chongqing University of Posts and Telecommunications</a>. During my MSc, I was supervised by <a class="academic-link" href="https://cs.cqupt.edu.cn/__local/4/7E/BA/09216308EDB7058F5C18383B164_59F28401_409B7.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Prof. Xianzhong Xie</a>.
